Supplier portal synchronization with Dynamics 365

CR-014Finding F-01Promise P4 · list v1{{status}}

Background

The Source-to-Pay scope for Atlas Industrial Group was priced on the assumption that the existing supplier portal stays outside Dynamics 365, with no integration work in the estimate.2 Discovery workshop WS-07 on 16 October established that supplier information and status must stay synchronized between that portal and Dynamics 365.3 This change request sets out what was sold, what was found, the proposed approach and the effort impact, so Atlas can decide.

Edited by Lina Haddad · Oct 20

What was sold

“The existing supplier portal remains outside the scope of Dynamics 365.”Proposal_Atlas_v3.pdf · §5 Assumptions · p.91 · estimate line “Supplier portal integration” priced at 0 days2

Recorded as promise P4 in the locked promise list (v1, 1 October 2026). No estimate line carries integration build, test or support for the portal.

What was found

“Supplier information and status must stay synchronized between the existing supplier portal and Dynamics 365.”WS-07 Supplier Management · p.3 · Rania Samir, Group Procurement Lead3

Recorded as finding F-01, confidence High. The technical label Integration and the match with P4 were confirmed by Omar Farid, Functional Consultant, on 20 October 2026.4

Proposed approach — pending Solution Architect validation

Not yet validated. Drafted from documentation and past delivery; Sara Mansour (Solution Architect) validates it before this pack is sent.

Supplier master data and status could be exchanged using standard integration options (data entities via OData; business or data events to notify the portal of changes).5 Which entities, fields and event types fit Atlas, and whether any extension is needed, must be validated by the Solution Architect.

Portal-side changes are required and sit with Atlas or the portal vendor: consuming the events, mapping supplier identifiers, and handling retries. Scope here covers the Dynamics 365 side, the interface contract, and joint testing.

Options for Atlas

Approve CR-014Synchronization delivered in this rollout · +18 person-days of work · timeline impact to be agreed with Atlas.
Defer to phase 2Go live with the portal unchanged. Supplier data is maintained in both systems until phase 2.
Keep manual processAtlas accepts manual maintenance of supplier records in the portal; a documented operating procedure is delivered instead.

What stays on record

What was originally sold stays on record: P4 v1 (Oct 1, 2026). If Atlas accepts, a new promise P7 “Supplier portal synchronization (CR-014)” is added in promise list v2. The original assumption is never overwritten.
