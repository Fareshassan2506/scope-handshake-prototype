Lessons for Pre-Sales

Future · Release 3
Fixes the cause, not only this project

At the end of Discovery, AI turns what Atlas taught us into suggestions for the next proposal. Pre-Sales reviews them before anything changes in the estimate template.

What Atlas showedSuggested change for the next proposalEvidence
